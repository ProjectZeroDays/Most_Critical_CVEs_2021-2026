# Critical Commercial & Military Avionics & Aeronautics Vulnerability Matrix (2021–2026)

## Key Takeaways & Exploit Vector Trends

1. **Strict Physical Domain Isolation vs. Connectivity Demands:** The primary challenge in aeronautical cybersecurity is maintaining rigid isolation between the **Aircraft Control Domain (ACD)**, **Airline Information Services Domain (AISD)**, and **Passenger Information & Entertainment Services Domain (PIESD)** amidst increasing demand for real-time connected aircraft telemetry.
2. **Legacy Protocol Cryptography Gaps:** Core aviation protocols like **ACARS, ADS-B, and ARINC 429** were designed before the threat of software-defined radio (SDR) exploitation. They largely lack origin authentication and end-to-end encryption, requiring wrapper security frameworks (such as ACARS Message Security - AMS).
3. **Software Safety Certification vs. Rapid Patching:** Because flight software must undergo rigorous safety certification (e.g., **DO-178C Level A**), deploying security patches to certified avionics components can take months or years, extending the operational window for known zero-day vulnerabilities.
